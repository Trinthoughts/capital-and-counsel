ABOUT

Understanding the deal behind the headline.

Capital & Counsel is an independent publication focused on the legal and commercial thinking behind major transactions and business developments.

The publication looks at M&A, private equity, financing and regulation through a practical question: why is this transaction structured this way, and what does that structure achieve?

Editorial approach

Analysis should distinguish reported facts from interpretation. Primary documents and reputable reporting should be used wherever possible, with claims attributed and sources identified.
