About Capital & Counsel

An independent legal and commercial publication exploring the law behind transactions, businesses and private capital.

FOUNDER & EDITOR

Isaac Lamy

Capital & Counsel was founded to explore the law behind transactions, businesses and private capital.

With a background in law and a particular interest in corporate law, M&A, private equity and financing, the publication examines how legal structures shape commercial outcomes and the work lawyers undertake behind major transactions.

The publication also covers private client, private wealth, real estate, tax and legal technology, with a focus on making complex legal and commercial issues clear, practical and accessible.

About the Publication

Capital & Counsel is an independent legal and commercial publication exploring the law behind transactions, businesses and private capital.

We examine developments across corporate law, M&A, private equity, financing, private client, private wealth, real estate, tax and legal technology, with a focus on how legal structures shape commercial outcomes.

Through transaction analysis, legal explainers and practical commentary, Capital & Counsel aims to make complex legal and commercial issues accessible while providing a closer look at the work lawyers undertake behind major deals and business decisions.
