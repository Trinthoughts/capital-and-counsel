About Capital & Counsel

Independent analysis of the legal and commercial mechanics behind major transactions.

FOUNDER & EDITOR

Isaac Lamy

Capital & Counsel was founded to explore the legal and commercial architecture of high value transactions.

With a background in law and a focus on private equity, M&A, financing, private client, private wealth and regulation, the publication examines how transactions are executed, structured and governed from term sheet to closing and beyond.

The aim is to provide clear, practical and rigorous analysis for students, professionals and anyone with an interest in the mechanics that drive modern transactions.
